Genshin passive_talent category design - process notes
(How we got from 3 raw planning docs to passive_talent_definitive_plan.txt,
kept for future reference - e.g. classifying a newly-released character's
Utility Passive later, or designing another deep/nuanced category.)

=== 1. Source gathering ===

Three source documents were involved, layered in over the course of the
design conversation:
- passive_talent_category_plan.txt - the initial flat proposal: wiki-sourced
  category definitions + member lists, with a "Miscellaneous" bucket for
  anything that didn't cleanly fit.
- passive_talent_table - a more granular version of the same data (e.g.
  Cooking Bonus broken down further into Adventurer's Dishes/ATK-Boosting
  Dishes/DEF-Boosting Dishes/etc.), used purely to CROSS-VALIDATE the first
  doc, not as a separate source of new categories.
- passive_talent_night_realm.txt - full per-character passive text for
  Natlan's Nightsoul-related mechanics. Requested to check one specific
  category swap (Phlogiston Talent -> Night Realm's Gift), but turned out
  to contain two ENTIRELY SEPARATE categories (Moonsign Benediction,
  Witch's Eve Rite) that hadn't been surfaced in either prior doc. Lesson:
  don't assume a source document only contains what it was fetched to
  answer - read the whole thing.

=== 2. Verification rigor applied throughout ===

Every proposed category, at every stage, went through the same checks
(scripted, not eyeballed) before being trusted:
- Every member name matched against the REAL roster (genshin_entities.json)
  - catches typos and non-existent characters immediately.
- Internal duplicate check within a single category's member list (caught
  "Ifa" listed twice in one draft).
- Cross-document consistency check when two sources described the same
  category (the plan.txt vs. passive_talent_table comparison, where every
  category's member set had to match exactly once the table's finer
  sub-splits were collapsed back to the plan's top-level buckets).
- Completeness check: does every real roster character appear SOMEWHERE
  across all categories + the Miscellaneous bucket? Run as an explicit
  script-based diff, not assumed. Caught nothing missing on the first two
  docs (a genuinely thorough manual research pass), and was re-run after
  the Miscellaneous edge-case pass too (25/25 accounted for, confirmed).
- Redundancy check against EXISTING dimensions already in the pipeline -
  the single most valuable check. Comparing a new category's member set
  directly against every existing attribute's values caught two things:
  - Night Realm's Gift's 11 members were IDENTICAL to region=Natlan -
    zero new puzzle-relevant signal, dropped entirely rather than kept
    for "completeness."
  - Moonsign Benediction's 10 members were ALMOST identical to
    region=Nod-Krai, except for one real exception (Zibai, region=Liyue) -
    kept, since that one exception means the category isn't actually
    redundant, it just mostly overlaps.
  General rule going forward: before finalizing a new category, diff its
  member set against every existing single-valued attribute (element,
  region, weapon, model, rarity, release_era, etc.). Exact or near-exact
  matches are a signal to question whether the new category adds anything,
  not just add it because a wiki page said it existed.

=== 3. Structural decisions ===

- Multi-valued from the start, once real overlaps were found (Lauma:
  Resource Finding + Wildlife; Mualani: Resource Finding + Phlogiston;
  Varesa: Movement SPD + Phlogiston) - this wasn't a hypothetical edge
  case, it showed up in the very first verification pass. Confirmed the
  backend already supports this pattern generically
  (categoriesForListAttribute, already used for "tags" per
  docs/ARCHITECTURE.md), so no new backend mechanism was needed.
- Thin siblings that are conceptually the SAME mechanic were merged into
  one parent (Forging Bonus [3] + Furnishing Creation Bonus [2] folded
  into Crafting Bonus [now 18]) rather than kept as their own
  barely-populated categories.
- Thin-but-conceptually-DISTINCT categories were kept separate even
  though both were on the small side (Stamina Consumption Reduction [13]
  vs. Movement SPD Increase [10]) - the deciding factor wasn't size alone,
  it was whether merging would erase a real distinction a knowledgeable
  player would care about. User's own reasoning: keeping them distinct
  "adds more depth... rewards those with deep game knowledge," which is
  the whole point of this category existing at all.
- Genuinely thin categories that don't merge into anything (Mora Cost
  Reduction Talent [2], Stellar Jubilee [4]) are still recorded in the
  data for completeness / in case future characters get added to them,
  but are expected to be silently excluded from ever becoming a live
  puzzle category via the same min-count-floor mechanism already
  established for local_specialty/boss_material/release_version - no new
  special-casing needed, just apply the existing floor.
- A near-miss consideration: whether Hexerei Character / Moonsign
  Benediction should be their OWN dimension separate from passive_talent,
  since they're conceptually different from "what does this Utility
  Passive DO out of combat" (they're closer to a faction/trait tag that
  enables a party-wide synergy state when 2+ members share it). Ultimately
  folded back into the single passive_talent dimension anyway for
  simplicity, but the distinction is worth remembering if a similar
  "faction tag" mechanic comes up again later - it's a real conceptual
  difference even if it ended up living in the same dimension here.
- Mechanical precision over surface-level similarity: Kachina's Turbo
  Whirly stamina reduction reads similar to Stamina Consumption
  Reduction, but was deliberately excluded from it because it's a
  personal/vehicle-scoped effect, not party-wide - the category's actual
  definition requires a party-wide effect. Don't include a character just
  because their ability sounds thematically similar; check the actual
  mechanical scope matches the category's definition.

=== 4. Resolving the Miscellaneous bucket (the ~25 wiki-unclassified characters) ===

Went character-by-character, not batch-guessed. For each:
- N/A: genuinely doesn't fit any existing category, left with no
  passive_talent value at all (a legitimate, expected outcome for a
  nullable multi-valued attribute - not every character needs one).
- Matched to an EXISTING category when the core MECHANIC lined up even if
  the flavor text or exact trigger differed from that category's other
  members - e.g.:
  - Freminet's Aquatic Stamina reduction -> Stamina Consumption Reduction
    (same mechanic, underwater-flavored variant).
  - Neuvillette's underwater Sprint SPD -> Movement SPD Increase (same
    mechanic, underwater-flavored variant).
  - Lynette (reveals Recovery Orb locations) and Nicole (reveals nearest
    chest via a compass-emulating passive) -> Resource Finding Talent,
    even though orbs/chests aren't traditional gatherable materials - the
    core "reveals a location on the minimap" mechanic is what matters,
    not the exact object type.
  Where a matched character's mechanic was distinct enough that the
  category's existing tooltip would misdescribe them, the tooltip itself
  was widened rather than the character left out - e.g. Escoffier's
  Cooking Mek ability isn't literally "a bonus dish from Perfect Cooking,"
  so Cooking Bonus's tooltip gained an "...or has a special cooking
  passive" clause to stay accurate for her too.
- Verified complete at the end (script-checked all 25 original entries
  had an explicit decision - N/A or a category match - nothing skipped).

=== 5. Tooltip-writing conventions settled on ===

- Match the site's existing single-sentence "This character ___." voice
  (see CategoryChip.tsx's other GENSHIN_DESCRIPTIONS entries).
- Explicitly include the word "passive" in the description text (not the
  category label/chip itself) - keeps chip labels short and scannable
  like every other dimension's, while still making clear the whole
  dimension is about Utility Passives specifically.
- Deliberately omit caveats from the source wiki text that are true of
  EVERY member of a category (or every Utility Passive generally, e.g.
  "only works while in the party and alive") - restating a universal
  truth in every tooltip doesn't help a player understand what makes
  THIS category distinct from the others.
- Deliberately simplify away mechanical nuance that doesn't affect
  category membership (Resource Finding's stacking rules, Cooking Bonus's
  per-character exact trigger conditions) - the tooltip communicates the
  theme, not an exhaustive rules explanation.
- Where a term genuinely isn't common player knowledge (Phlogiston,
  Stellar-Glimmer/Stellar-Conduct/Stellar-Swirl), keep the term itself in
  the tooltip (matches this category's whole "rewards deep game
  knowledge" premise) but pair it with a short in-line clarifier rather
  than assume familiarity.

=== Final result ===

11 categories, 102 distinct real characters covered (of 118), captured in
passive_talent_definitive_plan.txt. See that file for the actual member
lists, counts, and tooltip text to implement from.
