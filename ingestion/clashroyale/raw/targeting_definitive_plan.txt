Clash Royale — "Targeting" category — definitive plan
========================================================

Source of truth: raw/targeting.txt (hand-curated by the user, cross-
validated against every entity in output/clashroyale_entities.json —
0 typos, all 183 entities covered, either with a real value or explicitly
under the "No targeting" group).

POST-LAUNCH REVISION (after shipping): the 9 "Buildings + <secondary>"
dual entries below (Battle Ram/Evo, Suspicious Bush, Goblin Giant/Evo,
Ram Rider, Skeleton Barrel/Evo, Lava Hound) were REVERTED to
Buildings-only after real play surfaced a bad puzzle cell: "7 Elixir x
Targets Air & Ground" was solvable ONLY by Lava Hound, which nobody
would actually answer that way - Lava Hound's identity is "the buildings
card," and its Air & Ground classification depended on a secondary
death-mechanic, not its main attack. In-game, Clash Royale's own UI
lists only "Buildings" as the targeting for every one of these cards -
matching that established, recognizable convention beats being more
exhaustively "accurate" to secondary mechanics most players wouldn't
name as the card's answer. GOBLINSTEIN IS THE ONE EXCEPTION, kept dual
(Buildings + Air & Ground): it's a genuine two-entity card (the tanky
Goblinstein and the separate Goblin Doctor are both on the field
simultaneously, not a primary-card-transitions-to-secondary-mechanic
case like the other 9), so "Buildings" alone would be incomplete in a
way none of the reverted 9 actually were. The rest of this doc is left
as originally written (including the now-superseded per-card mechanic
reasoning for the 9 reverted cards) for historical context on WHY each
mechanic exists - just know the "Per-edge-case verdicts" section below
no longer reflects the shipped data for those 9 entries; see raw/
targeting.txt itself for the current, authoritative mapping.

Multi-valued attribute, same mechanism as Star Rail's affiliation:
AttributeContainsCategory, no min-count floor needed here (all three
values already have 28+ members). Attribute key: "targeting" (list[str]
on ClashRoyaleAttributes).

Core rule (the user's own words, this is what resolved every edge case)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
"A card must be able to TARGET, not merely be able to hit/affect."
A card's PRIMARY, ONGOING attack must actively select/pursue a category -
incidental splash damage within a melee/attack radius doesn't count
(Lumberjack's on-death rage splash hits air but Lumberjack only targets
ground; Evo Valkyrie's spin damages air troops caught in her radius but
she only targets ground). A ONE-TIME kit mechanic unrelated to the card's
primary/ongoing attack doesn't count either, even if the player can aim
it (Evo Cannon's one-time deploy splash can hit air, but this is the same
shape as Lumberjack's death-rage - a one-time event, not the card's
ongoing targeting behavior - so Evo Cannon stays Ground Only only,
explicitly NOT dual). A purely REACTIVE punishment effect doesn't count
either (Electro Giant's shock-back radius only fires when something else
attacks it - it never seeks out a target itself - so Electro Giant stays
Buildings only).

Spawner precedent (pre-existing, extended to the new edge cases)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
If a card spawns/carries a SEPARATE persistent, ongoing unit (a spawner
building's spawned troops, or a card that splits/transitions into other
persistent troops), that unit's own targeting also counts toward the
parent card - the exact same logic that already classifies Tombstone
(spawns Skeletons -> Ground Only) and Goblin Hut (spawns Spear Goblins ->
Air & Ground) as single-value entries, just extended to cards where the
secondary unit's targeting DIFFERS from the primary unit's, producing a
dual-value entry instead of a single one.

No "Ground Only" + "Air & Ground" together
---------------------------------------------
Decided during the discussion: since "Air & Ground" already implies
ground coverage, pairing it with "Ground Only" on the same card is
redundant/contradictory. Where a card's secondary unit adds air coverage
on top of a ground-only primary attack, the card gets ONLY "Air & Ground"
(not both) - this is why Night Witch and Goblin Machine are single-value
"Air & Ground" entries, not dual. The only dual combinations that exist
in the final data are "Buildings + Ground Only" and "Buildings + Air &
Ground" (Buildings is a fundamentally different targeting priority -
ignores troops entirely - so pairing it with either of the other two is
never redundant).

Per-edge-case verdicts (final)
----------------------------------
Goblinstein          -> Buildings + Air & Ground (two concurrent entities:
                         the tanky Goblinstein targets buildings, the
                         Goblin Doctor targets air & ground)
Night Witch           -> Air & Ground only (automatic ongoing Bat spawner
                         - not dual, per the no-redundancy rule above)
Goblin Machine        -> Air & Ground only (auto-targeting rocket
                         launcher is genuine ongoing targeting, not
                         splash - not dual, per the no-redundancy rule)
Evo Cannon             -> Ground Only only, NOT dual (one-time deploy
                         splash is the same shape as Lumberjack's
                         death-rage - doesn't count, see core rule above)
Electro Giant          -> Buildings only, NOT dual (shock-back is
                         reactive, not targeting - see core rule above)
Mother Witch            -> Air & Ground only, NOT dual (raised and
                         explicitly rejected post-launch). Her Hog Curse
                         is a real, automatic, ongoing part of her attack
                         - passes that bar - but the resulting Mini Hog
                         (Buildings-only) only spawns if a cursed troop
                         actually dies, which may be killed by something
                         else entirely and is not guaranteed to happen at
                         all in a given match. Every other dual case above
                         is a GUARANTEED, deterministic outcome of playing
                         the card as intended (Battle Ram's barbarians
                         WILL end up ground-targeting; Lava Hound's pups
                         WILL spawn on death) - Mother Witch's Buildings
                         behavior is conditional/probabilistic instead,
                         which would mislead players into expecting a
                         targeting behavior that may never manifest.
                         Decided to hold the line at "guaranteed on every
                         play," not just "automatic when it happens."
Hero Balloon           -> Buildings only, NOT dual (ability-spawned
                         Skeletrooper deliberately not extended dual
                         status, unlike the automatic-spawner cases)
Goblin Cage            -> Ground Only only (moved out of the "No
                         targeting" group into a real single value - the
                         base cage has no attack of its own, but the
                         spawned Goblin Brawler's ground-targeting counts
                         via the spawner precedent, same as Tombstone/
                         Goblin Hut)
Evo Goblin Cage        -> Ground Only only, unchanged (has its own real
                         pull+damage attack, already correctly single-
                         valued before this discussion)
Battle Ram/Evo         -> Buildings + Ground Only (barbarians persist as
                         independent ground troops after the ram phase)
Suspicious Bush        -> Buildings + Ground Only (spawns Bush Goblins)
Goblin Giant/Evo       -> Buildings + Air & Ground (spear goblins fire
                         concurrently, not just after death)
Ram Rider               -> Buildings + Air & Ground (her own ranged
                         attack + the ram's separate building-only charge)
Skeleton Barrel/Evo     -> Buildings + Ground Only (spawns Skeletons)
Lava Hound               -> Buildings + Air & Ground (splits into Lava
                         Pups on death)
Spirit Empress          -> Air & Ground (flying form)
Spirit Empress (Ground)  -> Ground Only (her separate ground ability form
                         - already a distinct entity in the data, not a
                         typo/duplicate)

No-targeting cards (excluded entirely, not a selectable category)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Clone, Elixir Collector, Mirror - these have no attack of their own at
all. Get an empty targeting list, same as any card the file doesn't
mention - NOT modeled as a literal "N/A" category value.

Card-type sanity cross-check (Troop x Building x Spell x Targeting)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Confirmed structurally correct, not a gap: no Building or Spell ever
appears under "Targets Buildings" (a stationary building can't target
another building, and a spell resolves at its cast location rather than
moving to attack one - only mobile Troops can hold this value).
Numbers below are POST the dual-category reversal above (9 cards moved
from a "Buildings + X" pair down to Buildings-only, so Air & Ground/
Ground Only each lost a few Troop entries versus the original count):
  Troops (150):   Buildings 28 | Air & Ground 60 | Ground Only 53
  Buildings (18): Buildings  0 | Air & Ground  4 | Ground Only 13 | none 1
  Spells (15):    Buildings  0 | Air & Ground 16 | Ground Only  7 | none 2
Furnace/Evo Furnace are card_type "Troop" (not "Building") - confirmed
with the user as an intentional post-launch rework, not a data bug.

Weight
-------
No weight tuning done yet in this plan doc - see the live-measurement
tuning report (mirrors Star Rail's starRailAffiliationTuningReport) run
before shipping, same process as every other multi-valued category in
this codebase.

Pipeline wiring
------------------
1. schema.py: added `targeting: list[str] = Field(default_factory=list)`
   to ClashRoyaleAttributes; reused existing warn_thin() for a thin-value
   warning (none fired - all three values are healthy).
2. normalize.py: parse_targeting() reads raw/targeting.txt at runtime
   (not a hand-typed dict - same reasoning as Star Rail's affiliation
   parser, targeting.txt is the actively-edited source of truth); looked
   up per-entity by its own display_name (not folded into map_card's
   shared base_attributes) since a form's targeting isn't guaranteed to
   match its base card's, even though it happens to today; validates
   every parsed card name resolves to a real entity (raises on typo).
3. ClashRoyaleGameModule.java: categoriesForListAttribute mirrors
   StarRailGameModule's, minus the minCount/weight params that dimension
   needed and this one doesn't.
4. CategoryChip.tsx: 3 CLASHROYALE_DESCRIPTIONS entries added, no icons
   (plain-text fallback pill, same as Star Rail's affiliation).
